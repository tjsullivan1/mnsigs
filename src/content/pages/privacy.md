## What we collect

This website is a static site. We do not run analytics, set cookies, or collect
personal information from visitors.

## Mailing list

If you choose to join our mailing list, your email address is collected and
stored by [Mailchimp](https://www.intuit.com/privacy/statement/), who process it
on our behalf. We use it only to send chapter news and event announcements. You
can unsubscribe at any time using the link in the footer of any email we send.

## Donations

Donations are processed by PayPal. We never see or store your payment details.
Please see [PayPal's privacy statement](https://www.paypal.com/us/legalhub/privacy-full)
for how they handle your information.

## Third-party links

Pages on this site link to third-party websites, including sigmachi.org. We are
not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

## Questions

If you have questions about this policy, please
[get in touch](/contact).
